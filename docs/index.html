Privacy Policy — Mushaf

Last updated: 2 May 2026

Mushaf (the "app") is published by Usama Rehman.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information. We do not use analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

Data stored on your device

The following data is stored only on your device, in app-private storage, using Android SharedPreferences and the application documents directory:

  • Your last-read page number
  • Your selected mushaf edition, font, and theme
  • Your bookmarks
  • A cached list of available audio reciters

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Network requests

When you select an audio reciter or refresh the reciter list, the app makes anonymous HTTPS requests to:

  • api.alquran.cloud
  • cdn.islamic.network
  • api.quran.com

These requests do not include any identifying information beyond what your device's HTTP stack normally sends (User-Agent, IP address). Audio files are streamed directly from those CDNs.

Children

The app contains no inappropriate content and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
